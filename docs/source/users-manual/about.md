## NGPS Overview

Next Generation Palomar Spectrograph (NGPS) is conceived to be a radically more efficient workhorse spectrograph, replacing the 1970s-era Double Spectrograph (DBSP). NGPS achieves these gains via 1) increased optical efficiency from superior design and grating components, 2) a novel focal plane slicer unit that captures most traditional slit losses, 3) increased operational efficiency due to broad spectral coverage with fixed grating format and a minimal set of user-selectable settings, and 4) improved scheduling and observing tools, and feedback.
